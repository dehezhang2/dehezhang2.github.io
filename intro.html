Can multimodal LLMs see in the dark?多模态大模型能在黑暗中看清吗？

The first benchmark for nighttime egocentric vision. Day–night aligned videos let the same question be asked under both lighting conditions, which improves annotation quality and isolates the effect of darkness. ConeGaussian

in submission投稿中

A pixel sees a bundle of rays, not a single ray.一个像素看到的是一束光线，而不是一条。

Anti-aliasing for Gaussian ray tracing on any calibrated central camera. The pixel footprint is built from the camera's own neighbouring rays and the ray–Gaussian response is filtered in closed form, so one filter serves pinhole and strongly distorted fisheye cameras.面向任意标定中心相机的 Gaussian 光线追踪抗锯齿。用相机自身的相邻光线构造像素 footprint，并以闭式形式滤波光线–Gaussian 响应，同一个滤波器同时适用于针孔和强畸变鱼眼相机。

StateSpaceDiffuser: long-term memory for diffusion world modelsStateSpaceDiffuser：给扩散世界模型加上长期记忆

NeurIPS 2025  Nedko Savov, Naser Kazemi, Deheng Zhang, Danda Pani Paudel, Xi Wang, Luc Van Gool

Diffusion world models predict the next frame from only the last few frames. Once a place leaves that window, it is forgotten, and returning to it produces a different scene. StateSpaceDiffuser adds a Mamba state-space branch that summarises the whole interaction history and conditions the diffusion model on it.扩散世界模型只根据最近几帧预测下一帧。一个地方一旦离开这个窗口就被遗忘，回到原处时会生成一个不同的场景。StateSpaceDiffuser 加入一个 Mamba 状态空间分支，概括全部交互历史，并用它来条件化扩散模型。

39.68 vs 26.13

average PSNR on MiniGrid recall with a 50-frame context, against the diffusion-only DIAMOND baseline (+51.9%). Human raters also prefer our CS:GO rollouts for long-context consistency.MiniGrid 回忆任务（50 帧上下文）的平均 PSNR，对比纯扩散基线 DIAMOND（+51.9%）。在 CS:GO 上，用户研究也更偏好我们的长程一致性。

What I took from it: the state is compressed into 256 dimensions and loses detail over long rollouts. A world model needs a persistent state, and I think the most useful one is explicit 3D.我的收获：这个状态被压缩到 256 维，长时间 rollout 会丢细节。世界模型需要一个持久的状态，而我认为最有用的状态是显式的 3D。

World models: learn the world from video, make it explicit in 3D世界模型：从视频中学习世界，再用 3D 把它显式化

Why start from video为什么从视频出发

  • Video is by far the most abundant data about the physical world, and it needs no 3D labels.视频是关于物理世界最丰富的数据，而且不需要 3D 标注。
  • To generate consistent video, a model has to capture parallax, occlusion, object permanence and plausible motion, at least partly.要生成一致的视频，模型必须（至少部分地）掌握视差、遮挡、物体恒存和合理的运动。

Why make it explicit in 3D为什么要显式成 3D

  • Pixels carry no metric scale, no object identity and no guarantee that the world persists (the StateSpaceDiffuser lesson).像素里没有尺度、没有物体身份，也不能保证世界是持久的（StateSpaceDiffuser 的教训）。
  • An agent needs something it can query and act on: distances, free space, contacts, what opens and how.智能体需要可查询、可操作的东西：距离、可通行空间、接触，以及什么能打开、怎么打开。

Reconstruct, then extract a surface先重建，再提取表面

video→pointmaps / depth→fusion→mesh

e.g. DUSt3R, VGGT, then TSDF, Poisson or 2DGS例：DUSt3R、VGGT，再接 TSDF、Poisson 或 2DGS

  • Anchored to the pixels, metric up to scale锚定在像素上，尺度可恢复
  • Only what was seen; noisy thin structures; one fused piece with no objects只覆盖看到的部分；细结构噪声大；融成一整块，没有物体
B

Generate natively in 3D直接在 3D 中生成

image(s)→3D latent→mesh

e.g. TRELLIS, Hunyuan3D例：TRELLIS、Hunyuan3D

  • Complete, clean, watertight shapes, hidden sides included形状完整、干净、封闭，包括看不到的一面
  • Mostly single objects from a few images; scenes, metric alignment and video input remain open多为少量图像下的单个物体；场景级、尺度对齐和视频输入仍未解决
C

Use the video model as a geometry prior把视频模型当作几何先验

video diffusion→depth / point videos→surface

e.g. DepthCrafter, GeometryCrafter, Aether例：DepthCrafter、GeometryCrafter、Aether

  • Temporally consistent geometry, with a prior learned from very large video data时间一致的几何，先验来自海量视频
  • Still 2.5D per frame; a surface and its structure must be recovered afterwards仍然是逐帧 2.5D；表面和结构需要事后恢复
My bet我的设想

Let observed pixels anchor what was seen, let the video prior generate what was not, and output an object-structured surface directly instead of fusing per-frame depth.让观测到的像素锚定看到的部分，让视频先验生成没看到的部分，并直接输出按物体组织的表面，而不是融合逐帧深度。

Test: does a video diffusion backbone trained to emit surface latents beat reconstruct-then-mesh on unseen regions and thin structures?检验：一个训练来输出表面 latent 的视频扩散主干，能否在未观测区域和细结构上胜过“先重建再提 mesh”？

VLMs

3D as a data engine3D 作为数据引擎

Render any viewpoint or trajectory of a reconstructed scene and compute answers from geometry: distance, size, direction, counting, routes. Labels are exact, difficulty is controllable, and an answer can be checked to stay the same across trajectories of the same scene.从重建的场景中渲染任意视角和轨迹，用几何直接计算答案：距离、尺寸、方向、计数、路径。标签精确，难度可控，还能检验同一场景不同轨迹下答案是否一致。

VLAs · robots

3D as a simulator3D 作为仿真器

Object-level scenes with collision and articulation turn captured places into environments where policies can be trained and evaluated, the real-to-sim direction we explore in PhiRIE. Drawers and doors only matter if they can open.带碰撞和铰接的物体级场景，可以把真实采集的地方变成训练和评测策略的环境，这正是我们在 PhiRIE 中探索的 real-to-sim 方向。抽屉和门只有能打开才有意义。

world models · agents世界模型 · 智能体

3D as persistent memory3D 作为持久记忆

Give a model an explicit scene state that it can query instead of re-perceiving every frame. This is the persistent state that StateSpaceDiffuser approximated with a compressed vector, made metric and inspectable.给模型一个可以查询的显式场景状态，而不是每一帧重新感知。这正是 StateSpaceDiffuser 用一个压缩向量近似的持久状态，只是变成了有尺度、可检查的形式。

The question I want to measure, not assume: which 3D fidelity does each consumer need? Metric geometry for measurement questions, object structure for counting and relations, articulation for manipulation.我想测量而不是假设的问题：不同的下游需要什么程度的 3D 保真？测量类问题需要尺度几何，计数与关系需要物体结构，操作需要铰接信息。
